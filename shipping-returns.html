Return & Refund Policy

Because our products fall into two categories — general-use devices and personal-contact wellness items — two different return paths apply.

Category A — Eligible for 60-Day Return

Applies to: RecoverPro40B, GlowMini60Pro, GlowE300, Solaris9 BK300 Executive Edition, LUFAMAX 1800, LUFAMAX 4800, LUFAESPRO 3000, and any other full-body panel not listed under Category B.

  • 60 days from the date of delivery to request a return.
  • Like-new condition required: unmarked, fully functional, with all original accessories, cables, manuals, and packaging.
  • A 15% restocking fee applies to approved returns, deducted from the refund. This fee covers inspection, sanitization, and refurbishment so the returned unit meets the same standard as new.
  • Return shipping is the customer's responsibility. Use a trackable, insured method — LUFALIGHT is not responsible for items lost or damaged in transit back to us.
  • RMA required. A Return Merchandise Authorization must be issued before shipping any item back; returns received without a prior RMA will not be accepted.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of the return passing inspection.

Category B — Final Sale Once Opened

Hygiene & personal-contact items. Applies to: GlowEM04, BeauMask, BeauMask + BeauNeck-Décolleté, VitalityPro PEMF Mat, LUMENOVA PRO, AuraDome, and ProStand.

Due to direct skin/scalp contact and the nature of these products, items in this category cannot be returned once the box has been opened — no exceptions for change of mind. This does not affect your warranty: a defective unit in this category is still fully covered under the manufacturer's warranty below.

Order Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours of purchase, provided the order has not yet shipped. Contact us immediately at lufalight@gmail.com with your order number.

Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. To exchange a product, return your eligible item under the Category A process above and place a new order for the item you'd like instead.

Damaged or Defective on Arrival

If your item arrives damaged or with a manufacturing defect, contact us within 7 days of delivery at lufalight@gmail.com with photos or video of the issue. We will arrange a free replacement or repair at no cost to you — no restocking fee applies to verified defects.

How to Start a Return

  1. Email lufalight@gmail.com with your order number and reason for return.
  2. We'll confirm eligibility and issue your RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) with return shipping instructions.
  3. Ship the item back within 14 days of receiving your RMA using a trackable, insured carrier.
  4. Once received and inspected, we'll process your refund within 5–7 business days.

Questions about a specific product's return eligibility? Contact us at lufalight@gmail.com — we're happy to help before you buy.
