What's Not Covered

  • Damage from misuse, drops, water exposure, or unauthorized modification/repair.
  • Normal cosmetic wear (scuffs, scratches) that does not affect function.
  • Devices purchased from unauthorized third-party resellers.

How to Make a Warranty Claim

  1. Email lufalight@gmail.com with your order number, a description of the issue, and photos/video if possible.
  2. Our support team will troubleshoot with you — many issues can be resolved without a return.
  3. If a repair or replacement is needed, we'll send a prepaid shipping label (Canada & USA) for the defective unit.
  4. A repaired or replacement device is shipped out once the original is received, typically within 5–10 business days.

Extended Care

Need help beyond the warranty period? We offer affordable out-of-warranty repair quotes — email us with your device model and the issue and we'll get back to you within 1-2 business days.

For information about returning a recent purchase for a refund instead, see our Shipping & Returns page.
